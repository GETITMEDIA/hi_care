ANTI RUST CAOATING

Car bodies are primarily made of cast iron and high-strength steel, making them prone to rust when exposed to moisture and air. Rusting, or corrosion, weakens the metal and affects vehicle longevity. Anti-rust coating provides essential protection, especially for underbody parts. Helps prevent long-term damage and maintains structural integrity.
